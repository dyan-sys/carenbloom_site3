Four Areas

One standard. Four categories.

01
Sexual health

Care for intimacy, hormones, fertility, pleasure, and agency still asks people to navigate stigma. We build brands that make the category easier to enter and safer to trust.

02
Longevity

Longevity is moving from abstract promises to practical healthspan: energy, recovery, prevention, metabolic health, and routines people can keep.

03
Mental health

People need support that feels accessible and human, with enough clinical seriousness to be trusted and enough cultural fluency to be used.

04
Education

Better health decisions start with language people understand. Education turns expert knowledge into routines, confidence, and behavior change.

What We Value

How we raise the ceiling.

01
Outsized ambition

We set targets that feel uncomfortable. If the goal doesn't create tension, it isn't big enough to change the trajectory.

02
Agency

We hire people who move before being asked. Ownership means you see the gap, fill it, and tell us after.

03
Reduce cycle time

Speed is a value, not a tactic. Shorter loops between decision and result mean faster learning and fewer wasted months.

04
Relentless upskilling

Every quarter, the bar moves. We invest in people who invest in themselves — and we expect the gap between today and last quarter to be visible.

05
Organic (re)org

Roles evolve as the company evolves. We don't protect titles — we protect momentum. The best people end up where they create the most value.

06
Human potential

We believe most people are capable of far more than they've been asked to do. We ask more.

07
Creativity

Original thinking is a competitive advantage. We reward ideas that are surprising, specific, and brave enough to be wrong.
