# Questions for Ivan — v3 Direction

## Goal
Get enough signal to pick a design direction without sounding directionless.
Frame: "I have the v2 baseline — tell me what to push forward and what to rethink."

---

## Draft Message

Hey Ivan, before I start on the redesign I want to make sure we land somewhere you're happy with. A few quick things:

1. **What bothered you most about the v2 site** (aside from the similarity issue)? Anything about the layout, tone, or feel that you'd want different this time?

2. **From the inspo sites you shared — what caught your eye?** Not asking you to pick one, just curious if it was the visuals, the way content was structured, the overall vibe, etc.

3. **Who's the main audience for this site?** Are we speaking more to potential clients, partners, or talent? That'll shape how we write and what we lead with.

4. **Is there a specific action you want visitors to take?** (Book a call, apply, learn about services, etc.)

---

## Why these questions work

- Q1: Gives you concrete things to avoid/improve — he'll have opinions here because v2 existed.
- Q2: Narrows the inspo without asking him to design it. You're asking *why* he liked them, not *which one to copy*.
- Q3 & Q4: Visionary but practical. These are founder-level questions that show you're thinking about purpose, not just pixels.

## What to avoid asking
- "Which inspo site should we follow?" — too passive
- "What pages do you want?" — too tactical, he'll expect you to figure that out
- "Can you describe your ideal site?" — too open-ended, he'll disengage
